## ✨ Overview

**IronCore** is a gym management system built to bring the main operations of a fitness center into one organized platform.

The system provides role-based functionality for gym staff and members, helping manage **memberships, trainers, workout plans, attendance, members, and daily gym activities** through dedicated interfaces.

---

## 🚀 Main Features

* 👑 **Admin Management**
* 🏋️ **Trainer Management**
* 🧑‍💼 **Reception & Check-in**
* 💪 **Member Management**
* 📋 **Workout & Exercise Plans**
* 📅 **Attendance Tracking**
* 💳 **Membership & Plans**
* 👥 **Staff & User Management**
* 🔐 **Authentication & Role-Based Access**
* 📊 **Dashboard & Statistics**
* ⚙️ **System Management**

---

## 👥 User Roles

| Role                   | Responsibilities                                                   |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 👑 **Admin**           | Manage gym operations, users, trainers, members and system data    |
| 🏋️ **Trainer**        | Manage exercises, workout plans and assigned members               |
| 🧑‍💼 **Receptionist** | Handle check-ins, members and front-desk activities                |
| 💪 **Member**          | View membership, exercises, plans and personal fitness information |
| 👤 **Guest**           | Explore the gym and access public information                      |

## 🧩 Core Modules

### 📊 Dashboard

Provides an overview of gym activities and important management information.

### 👥 Member Management

Manage gym members, membership information, and member-related activities.

### 🏋️ Trainer Management

Organize trainers and their assigned fitness-related responsibilities.

### 💪 Exercises & Workout Plans

Create and manage exercises and workout plans for gym members.

### 🛎️ Check-in

Manage member attendance and daily gym check-ins.

### 💳 Membership Plans

Organize available membership plans and related information.

### 🔐 Authentication

Role-based authentication controls access to the appropriate areas of the system.
